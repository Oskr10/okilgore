Overview

A WordPress website for a professional bark blowing company serving North Idaho and Spokane-area customers.

Site goal

Organize several different service and product messages into a clear path toward requesting an estimate while establishing the regional service coverage of the company.

Visual direction

The site uses large landscape photography, natural tones and alternating content sections to balance service information with examples of finished landscaping environments.

Final experience

My role

I designed and built the WordPress website, creating the responsive layouts and organizing the service, product and lead-generation content.

Result

The finished site brings the service areas, bark options and quote request into one path, so a visitor arriving from any page can reach an estimate request.
